Problem

The cost

Data is the bottleneck

Rich labels are scarce and costly, so smaller teams cannot afford them.

The precedent

Explain the driving, not just do it

NVIDIA’s Alpamayo-R1 trains on driving reasoning, built with auto-labeling plus human review.

Our bet

Label scenarios at scale

A VLM turns driving video into labeled scenarios, so datasets cost less to build.

Sources: Mordor Intelligence, Waymo paper, NVIDIA dataset cards, Alpamayo-R1 (arXiv 2511.00088). $5 figure is indicative.

Why this should work

Recent NVIDIA work supports auto-labeling plus judge-based data selection

Auto-labels at scale

Big model labels, small model learns

Alpamayo 2 Edge (2B parameters) is distilled from Chain-of-Causation autolabels made by the larger Alpamayo 2 Super.

Judges pick the data

Mid-range scores teach the most

A human-calibrated judge scores reasoning. Resampling by score lifted the mid-score share of a curated set from 27% to 81%.

What we borrow

Label, judge, then select

Our QA judge plays that role: score labels against camera frames, then curate clips by score.

Source: NVIDIA Developer Blog post on running AV reasoning models within onboard compute and latency limits.
