Input and output: what Waymo gives us, and what we add

The dataset

Waymo Open Dataset: real driving segments recorded by Waymo’s self-driving cars. Non-commercial research licence.

One sample

A 20-second segment: five cameras plus LiDAR at 10 Hz. We use the front camera only.

Waymo’s own labels

Boxes around vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists, plus time of day and weather per segment.

Per-frame output fieldIn Waymo already?What we add
time of day, weatherYes, segment statsVLM predicts per frame; scored against Waymo
pedestrian, cyclist visibleYes, via object boxesVLM predicts per frame; scored against the boxes
road typeNoNew enrichment, not scored. e.g. highway, urban street, residential, parking lot
ego motionNo label, only speedNew label, reference derived from speed. e.g. stopped, accelerating, braking, cruising
changed vs previous frameNoNew, from the temporal variant (C). e.g. pedestrian_visible: false → true

Source: waymo.com/open/about. Perception data: 5 cameras and LiDAR, 20-second segments at 10 Hz, with 2D and 3D boxes for vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists.
